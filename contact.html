About Us

It’s not easy to find your favorite local snacks made the way they should be. Too often, street vendors cut corners — using cheap, greasy oils, ignoring hygiene, and serving food that feels heavy, oily, and unsatisfying. You leave with a bad taste in your mouth, both literally and figuratively.

At Parsa Refreshment, we set out to change that. We believe your snacks should be a treat, not a compromise. That’s why we prepare every samosa, pakora, jalebi, and weekend special with premium Dalda Banaspati — ensuring they’re perfectly crispy, light, and full of flavor without the greasy after-feel.

We don’t just stop at taste. Cleanliness and quality are at the heart of everything we do. From carefully selected ingredients to strict hygiene practices, we make sure every bite is one you can enjoy with complete peace of mind.

Because for us, it’s not just about food — it’s about giving you the joy of enjoying your favorite snacks the way they were meant to be: fresh, hygienic, and unforgettable.

With Parsa Refreshment, you’re not just eating — you’re reliving the authentic taste of tradition, done right.
